How Fundzy works.

A simple path from discovering a scholarship to preparing your application. Fundzy helps you understand information; the official provider handles the application and final decisions.

01

Discover opportunities

Search the directory by scholarship name, provider, state, study level or course-related keywords. Explore national and selected state-level guides.

02

Review eligibility

Read the eligibility pointers and scheme notes. Compare them with your actual situation and confirm every condition in the current official guidelines.

03

Prepare documents

Use the preparation checklist as a starting point. The official scheme notice determines which certificates and formats are required.

04

Visit the official portal

Continue to the provider's website to submit the application. Fundzy does not submit forms, upload documents or approve awards.

05

Track your progress

Use My Tracker to save scholarship names, notes, preparation steps and your application status in your current browser.

06

Ask Fundzy AI

Ask questions in English, Hindi, Hinglish or other languages. AI guidance is informational; verify important details with official sources.

Keep your information safe

Never share Aadhaar numbers, bank account details, passwords, OTPs or sensitive documents in the chatbot. Do not rely on a chatbot answer as a final eligibility decision.
