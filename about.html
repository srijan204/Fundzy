ABOUT THE PROJECT

Scholarship info, minus the overwhelm.

Fundzy is an independent student information project designed to help students across India discover scholarship programmes, understand eligibility pointers, prepare documents and reach official application portals.

Our purpose

Reduce the effort it takes to find scholarship information and understand the next steps.

02

Our approach

Present programme guides, eligibility pointers, document preparation notes and official links in one student-friendly place.

03

Our boundary

Fundzy is not a government portal, does not submit applications and cannot guarantee eligibility or scholarship awards.

Data accuracy matters

Scholarship availability, deadlines, eligibility rules and documents can change. Catalogue entries are snapshots and may include renewal-only opportunities. Always check the latest notice on the official provider's website before applying.

Fundzy is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any government department, scholarship authority or educational institution.
